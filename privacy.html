Privacy Policy

Applies to: Blokzen, Sortzen and Mergezen (the “games”), published by MSOGUZ. Effective: 6 October 2026.

What the games store

  • An anonymous player ID created automatically by Firebase Authentication the first time a score is sent. It is not linked to your name, e-mail, phone number or device advertising ID.
  • Your nickname – generated automatically, and changeable by you.
  • Your scores and the time they were submitted, for the daily, weekly and all-time leaderboards.

Game progress and settings are kept only on your device.

What the games do not do

  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking across apps or websites.
  • No access to contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.
  • No sale or sharing of data with third parties.

Where it is stored

Leaderboard data is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, EU multi-region) and transmitted over HTTPS. Google acts as a data processor and may log technical data such as IP addresses to operate and protect the service. See Firebase privacy information.

Who can see it

Your nickname and scores are visible to other players on the leaderboards. Do not use a nickname that identifies you if you do not want that.

Retention and deletion

Leaderboard entries are kept while the leaderboards exist. To have your entries and anonymous ID deleted, e-mail msoguz44@gmail.com with your nickname and the game; requests are handled within 30 days. Deleting the game from your device removes all locally stored data.

Children

The games contain no ads, purchases or chat and do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

Updates to this policy will be published on this page.

Sıralama verileri Google Firebase'de (Cloud Firestore, AB çoklu bölge) saklanır ve HTTPS ile iletilir. Google veri işleyen olarak hizmeti işletmek ve korumak için IP adresi gibi teknik verileri kaydedebilir.
